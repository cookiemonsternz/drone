Its now the next day (I took a nap and then didn't wake up lol, sorry soup) and I've been doing a bit of further research into vtx stuff. My current solution is a tx5823 module, which has the downside of being extremely big. It's 22x19mm, which is pretty much the entire size of the board itself (25.5x25.5mm). This is a bit of a problem, as I'd need to basically add another layer to the stack, which wouldn't be ideal structurally.

I've been looking at other options and the rtc6705 is also possible, but this would also be quite costly, as the chip is quite hard to source (again probs just aliexpress and all the ones i'd trust are like 20 dollars). Because of this, I'm seriously considering just using an external vtx. It's pretty much what I'm doing already, the tx5823 is pretty much just a vtx module with 1 extra step (capacitors) so it's not really sacrificing any effort on the project.
