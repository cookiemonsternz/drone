# Drone Journal 18 - 24/04/2026

Going through pcb layout and it is incredibly obvious that this will not fit. Need to optimise a bit for size and the BECs are looking like prime targets.

First of all, with that vtx chip I was eyeing up, I don't actually need a 10v output, as it runs off of 5v. I do think I'll have a seperate dc-dc converter just for electrical isolation, but I definitely don't need a 2A bec, as the vtx module only uses 180mA.

And heres the new 5V vtx BEC

Speaking of shrinking things, I can decrease the rated voltages on the 5v bec capacitors a bit, and probably optimise the inductors a lot.

Obviously the new inductor is way smaller in inductance and thus can be way smaller, down to just an 0805 chip inductor still with 2A max current.

I've also decided to just use two of the same circuit for both the BECs, theres really no disadvantages tbh

And after all of those changes this is the current state of the thing:
