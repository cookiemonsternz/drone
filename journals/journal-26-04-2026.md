# Drone Journal 20 - 26/04/2026

Welp guess whos back? Its me :)

CAD work today; I've decided to build my own frame instead of buying, and get it printed somewhere out of resin instead of fdm.

For CAD, i'm basing it off the betafpv air 65 II, as thats what I designed my pcbs around.

Heres my required feature set / design requirements:
- 25.5mm mounting holes
- Prop guards
- Mounts for 1102 / 1103 motors (12000-14000kV)
- 40-ish mm props
- Mount for 450-650mAh lipo, 2s
- Very crashable
- Camera mount with adjustable angle (~5-40 degrees probs)

For mounting the electronics stack, I want not too much support, as some springiness will help absorb impacts and not damage the electronics on a crash. Does need to be semi rigid though, otherwise the gyro and other stuff won't work perfectly.
