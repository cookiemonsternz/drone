Honestly pretty decent results :)
The carbon fibre obviously looks waay better than the plain nylon, but this sim hasn't taken into account weaknesses along layer lines, which is much more of a problem in fdm prints than mjf. In terms of breaking, I would say it is possible at 10m/s and quite likely at 15m/s, which should be around the top speed of the drone.

I'd like to also quickly model another couple types of collisions, although the above one is probably most common, it'll also be pretty common to land reasonably hard on the battery. Upside down might also be a thing but it'll be tricky to model without the pcbs there
