Done some material research and I've decided to go with PA12-HP Nylon, as this has really good mechanical properties. Resin is too brittle and will likely just shatter on impact, which is very bad for reusability. FDM can't get the resolution I need consistently (~2mm walls) and I can get it for only ~$4 (from jlc3dp) anyway, so it's not ridiculously expensive. With my current design, the frame would weight around 11g, which is very acceptable.
